GIF demonstrations were encoded to H.264 MP4 (yuv420p, CRF 23, faststart) without changing their content. WebP posters use the first frame. The shape-guide image was encoded to WebP at quality 90. Capture MP4 and game artwork are unchanged originals.

Videos are silent demonstrations, with native playback controls, no autoplay, and `preload="none"`. No LinkedIn embeds, expiring CDN URLs, or social tracking scripts are needed at runtime. The Godot live demo is opt-in.
